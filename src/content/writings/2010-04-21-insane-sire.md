Sire, isn’t it too late
To heal the cracks,
Clot the ooze,
From the ruptured capillaries
Of the neglected citizens?

Come out from the comfy cushion
To the end of the courtyard
And witness the cloud rushing with horror,
Visit the rivulets near by….
Bearing valued sweat and toil.

Ask the black-topped tracks on cliffs
About the hands that bled
They build them; you drive today.

Ask your insanity…….
Do the kins of the blistered hands
Enjoy the legacy too?
What justice,
Your psyche rewarded them with?
Listen the air and aver the truth.
Your stooges befooled you.
Benevolent Sire,
Isn’t it vain
To retain the text of
The Royal plural ‘We’…?

The abandoned souls
And the expectant world
Say;
‘Your insanity be punished,
Your ears be unwaxed,
A school be built
For you alone…
The farsighted Sire!!
