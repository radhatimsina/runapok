With my eyes tired and dried
reminiscence limited in mind
Unfinished are my most works,
and, those plans
we had together coined;
it’s true
Your absence is duly felt.
Perhaps, thus;
You trail me in all my walk and work,
At times
your silhouette startles
in apologetic
and, in pensive mood
You appear with smile
In my every dream.
I am happy
I enjoy your company even as a spirit.
See,
much water has flown under the Torsa Bridge
and, hordes of meadow grown
around thy garden of remembrance;
yet, you haven’t stopped appealing me.
Why?
you have your share in my heart
and much more desires in due,
our amity hasn’t gone with your Go
nor the love is lost.
I only have a wish-
Hello, non-existing Dear
Reincarnate being my son…
together we shall execute
and accomplish our due plans then.
