![](/images/wp/2009/12/images-25.jpg)

Is as in desert, marooned souls subsist here
Is far below the modern horizon.
As of cacti, lives are in array there
As to camel, resources proficiently burdened
Souls smile, willy-nilly, in a Dickensian slum.

![](/images/wp/2009/12/images-28.jpg)

Amidst, thousands as of cacti’s pronounced premises
A composed lotus bloomed being beyond desert’s quota
Illuminated the, otherwise, neglected oasis
Induced sanguine hope, albeit, in iota
Sure a challenge, to the natural science.

![](/images/wp/2009/12/images-27.jpg)

Fallow silica layer gathered potent dominance
Frost with air made its route thro’
Meek lives, then, fueled with exotic exuberance
Mirage lost its way, rainbow- taming loo
Souls gilded perchance from nearby shore.

![](/images/wp/2009/12/images-26.jpg)

The lotus parted fragrance in the ether,
Tactfully, winged arthropods drowned in frisson
Aftermath, birds seen flying hither and thither.
A conducive upbringing, flora and fauna discerned
Suffered lives; by degrees, seem amicably suit.
Sultry desert blessed with apt green house,
Sterile field turned duly fertile,
Cacti bore broad leaves and branched
Cursed arena, remained no more docile
Sui generic lotus, then, yielded grains so sweet.
