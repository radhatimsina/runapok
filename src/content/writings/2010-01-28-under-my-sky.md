The Red sun;
With hidden brilliance
Appeared at the distant horizon
At around five in the morning;
The crescent moon
In her silent move-
Seemed witnessing start of the day
Competing sun- just ahead.
Just at about in two shakes of a lamb’s tail
The lunar figure vamoosed;
The lightener glossed.

The green trees shone
Birds could be seen
Perched on the ‘spring’ twigs
Perhaps, to fly searching prey
For the progeny back in the nest.

![](/images/wp/2010/01/images-14.jpg)

I witnessed- nature in full
Over a cup of black tea
Sitting on the patio
Of a forested cottage.
And could read the scene
\-somewhat ominous
\-as though complaining
\-some how thirsty
In the vein of crested serpent
Searching woodpecker made holes
To quench the call for apt pap and water.
To add to my surprise,
Dark patch of cloud seen
Rushing from Northern- range
And towards the south
As if to stop early morning rays
Warm my forecourt
Clandestinely in mute moves
Suffocating corruptly misused
– otherwise cute earth.

And my mind captured visages
Of my loved land’s map
\-seen something missing
Of course, required
To call her the cloud nine
For all dwellers all times.
I then entered the cottage
Anticipating the vamoosed
Lunar figure’s reappear
Anon.
