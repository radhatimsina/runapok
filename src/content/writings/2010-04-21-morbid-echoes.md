I feel to curse my fate
And feel to blame myself
For having engraved
Empty urn within my crappy being.

Having attended
All days in life
With nil balance
In the sake of self
For their tomorrow
And,
In the name of
Being a man
For humanity
I feel to curse my fate.

In queue in mind
I have pious dreams
Awaiting safe landing,
But, I the bearer
Bewildering on the fence.

Having much pelf
Having a comfy life,
re at all not my intentions
Nor am an ineffectual being.

To bring to an end
All, round the clock misery
I have no avarice towards
Ones well-beingness,
Or have a sense of revenge- taking .
I am water. My mind is water.
No one can sink my differences
Rather I smirk with no means.

![](/images/wp/2010/04/images-9.jpg)

I am already on the mid-road
Titivating my deserted life track
No one can be stemming
For I can see a glossy light
At the end of the tunnel.
