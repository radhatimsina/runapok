Drank… drank and you drank
Thirst of it nearly drank you to whole
Had all been gathered, would fill a tank
For years you took it for granted, life’s goal.

![](/images/wp/2009/12/images-21.jpg)

Began with cock-a-doodle-doo till where’s my nest-
You dived in; you lost with and got bliss in the mug
No time sailed to you to ponder, nor wisdom sought rest
Sane part of yours ditched, so worse to liver bug.

Thank God! Thank Him. Who saw you on the foot
Kudos to your fate, for timely hearing the nth knock
Hope in life seen in your eyes gave instant true root
Smile thus spread seeing you busy in all dawn to dusk.

Your stiffened veins took their course, physique so suit
Life’s wheels on true will…so good in look
Your kiths & kins and fans patted you, for devils quit
Knave turned the salt of the earth no more crook.

You are to use the gotten fate with caution and trust
For all alone the ball is rolling in your court
The devilish fluid may again entice you for unseen lust
Build up within you the esteem to rescind you to comfort.

![](/images/wp/2009/12/images-20.jpg)

The lines on the walls, if not read true to their meaning
And the road in front is not taken to its true destination
May you load odds and stumble while toddling
Boat is ready at shore; get into, sail towards perfection.
