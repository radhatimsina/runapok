I myself don’t know an answer for a question-
How I am tagged refugee?
June 20th knocks my mind each year
For, if I have gotan answer.
I ponder-
I was too young to commit a crime then-
Enough to qualify for the Refugee Tag

![](/images/wp/2015/06/images.jpg)

I am unaware of any natural disaster-
That ruined my hamlet and pushed me so far.
Two decades after, Today;
I little realized-
It was calculated and served-
To aiding bliss for someone
Living still, with head hold high
Resilience- that is what my pride
I am letting Today, to pass by-
With sanguine hope,
My guiding Resilience shall inspire Him-
Rethink, apprehend and make free-
The following year…
My long caged bliss and dreams
That shall shower on Him the real bliss-
For I am not born Refugee!!!
Coz. It’s the 21st Century.
