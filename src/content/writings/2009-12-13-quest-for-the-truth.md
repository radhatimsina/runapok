Yet,
Hope smiles within her,
She sees a ray,
Albeit at the end of tunnel
Makes her mind to seek ease aptly.
“At the cost of all horrific tracks,
I must keep my soles on
I’m deprived; I’m a made fall guy
I must heal the wound;
I’m in quest for the truth indeed.
My foot of course, on peril
Come hell or high water.”

![](/images/wp/2009/12/images-24.jpg)

Although with the memento in front
\-She whispers. Yet, wreaks no relief.
With heart full of love, lips chapped;
Feels alone on ma’s lap…
Looks up and asks the firmament,
“Share me the nitty-gritty of the cause!
I’ve intrepid mind in comfy haven,
Nor am I at all in straits,
Help me, clear the mist,
End my morose state.”
Then, recalls unseen tears in spate and deluge
“Both are great, oh God! I’m unripe.
Erudite souls with erroneous claim
Survive at miles apart.
Yet,
With their hearts glued.
I’m deprived of;
Fatherly love—
Parental affections-
I’m made a scapegoat
Having right to read the cause,
I’m at the star- crossed state.
Remorse smiles on both the faces
And my heart wails;
Hearts I see battling
All against the vicissitudes of theirs,
Oh my Maker!
Help, unfurl the mystery.”
With a grain of hope
She prays;
“My Maker,
Render me spirit to unearth the Truth
And fulfill mu quest,
Before my end on Earth
Let me endeavor
For a retie
Of, the then,
Untied martial knot.
