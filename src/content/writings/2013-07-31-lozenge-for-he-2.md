When commitment is distorted
And the words are made derailed,
Illusion smiles and trust lightens
Hope withers and progress declines.

When personal egoism is well laid
And a self-great declaration made
Then the naive soul and body mingle
The bond weakens to live single.

When greed is prayed for and gotten
And needs of another one are forgotten
The self-heads towards uncertainties
The scum of the Earth’ is undoubtedly he.

When wit is not timely updated
And all hearsays undertaken related
Stands indecisive and pretends in vacuum he hears
On non-progressive wheels he must adhere.

When enough is adorned
And his kith and kin are ignored
Locked up inside his head he nourishes
A knavish brain -where consanguinity finishes.

When decentralization is erased
And supremacy empowered, and the praised
Tries bleaching the veins bluish
He’s a leech-like man myopically selfish.

When life is well defined
And a true morale is coolly coined
He sees all faces pious and pure
The salt of the Earth’ everyone’s cure.
