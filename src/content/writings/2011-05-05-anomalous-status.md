I am a man as of your stature,
I do have kids and other half
Carrying me within them
As is true to you and all mankind.
Nonetheless,
I am under oath for sovereign land;
I am a knight behind the shining armour
Solely dyed in the wool for the disadvantaged ones.
I guard the masses even in an untoward vista
I have schooled myself with the notion:
‘Live and let live’ and advocate and practise the same.
Is my obligation- I guard you
Is my commitment- I help you
Is my ethics- I salute you
Do jog your memory- I too arrest you.

I get from what you pay
I give what you need
I pass sleepless night and spare you sound sleep
I am a man as of your stature
Call me no more Jekyll and Hyde
For I have been sworn for land’s entity.
At times I am a cop even to my other half
And equally a beau whilst in civil robe.
In uniform no being is near and dear to my lens.
Pa and mamma, be general for I am under oath.
