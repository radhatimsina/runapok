The Maker so great,
Pioneered our ways,
And, then- let us grow.
He is immiscible;
He is too small to see
Yet, he is omnipresent

Lease His faith- say Him

![](/images/wp/2013/07/biswa-ijtema-prayer-akheri-monajat.jpg)

we owe you much.
Many say-
‘He is the cause of all the causes’
He sends and tests with different ruckus
To comprehend
How witty are his made?
How artistically his wish instigated?

Thus,
the commotion we are with-
‘re the lessons taught to us
Lease His faith- say Him
we’re so indebted.

Inducing skills and aptitudes within us
Watching He is. Perhaps
Likely Misuse- His big worry.
Guns and bombs may we are making
To ruin his entire Made
He curses nev’ –but, For sure
May punish in some way

How about;

Do away with guns and bombs

Lease His faith- say Him
we love each other.
