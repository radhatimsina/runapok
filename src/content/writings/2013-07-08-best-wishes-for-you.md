Sure baby, you win safe tracks
All ados, let run away and lost
Gather ointment to heal others’ cracks
That’s my wish cost.

I wish you be a good doctor
All needy, longing you be so
Give them the blessed nectar
Obligation you owe.

![](/images/wp/2013/07/images-11.jpg)

Life ain’t easy, there are cliffs and hills
Anyway march on; sea has shore and ship
Do always raise hands towards penurious  and ills
Gear-up your hardship.

On earth, many people come and go
Very few go remaining alive
I wish baby! you’ve the will to show
Care all in human hive.

Weigh your pen and tongue ever
another wound may it then make
Elsewhere- this has been modern fever
Always you remain awake.

Even a lovely Rose of one day
Drops and then decay alone
Attention!  Life has it in line; the same way
Try giving all possible best anon.

![](/images/wp/2013/07/images-12.jpg)

Let me see and hear you giving
With all possible values from your bag,
Helping all needy, for great doing
Receive my blessed tag-

Best wishes for you…
