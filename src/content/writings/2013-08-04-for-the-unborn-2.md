In an early dawn
of a late autumn
through a window
of a semi-urban house
dropped a letter.

![](/images/wp/2013/08/photo.jpg)

The lady of the flat
picked it up,
Yet;
the dropper remained unknown.
She ran through the lines…..
“Seela, love and soothing kisses to you
though late.
I’m at the verge of
breathing my last…
I’m wounded
to no repair.
I did my best
to let my land live long.
I am happy indeed,
for I’ve lessen
the risk of being fallen.

Take care of the fetus,
it has my blood
your only living hope.

The name tag for it
that we have chosen
Sure, you have taken due care.
I had thought of soiree with cronies
to confer its named-tag
by the gender betwixt the two
I could not make it,
Seela, my humble plea,
do confer from my behalf too.

May they bring to you
my stiffened corpse …
with wilted rugs
My humble plea!
please have a snap
even of my corpse,
for I haven’t had any.
Do it just for the sake of
our progeny, dear!
to imagine my once existence.
Be in good heart
and moist not the eyes,
I am parting,
Martyrdom owes…
Seela, love and soothing kisses to you
shall shower by my soul
take care of the unborn”.
