The seed sown decades afore
Duly suffered horrendous seasons
Awaited apt conditions to befall,
Sought apt humus in close proximity,
Ruined, testa and the hilum too;
Yet, the embryo survived.
Of late, perhaps with modest wish
Surprisingly has germinated.

![](/images/wp/2009/11/images-14.jpg)

Radical has well gripped the substratum,
The plumule risen on sight
Is lucky. For being dicotyledonous…
Exotic fertilizers need not fall,
For it has developed tap root,
Secondary roots with fertile nodules.
Thank nature. The nitrogen fixing bacteria
Housed there.

![](/images/wp/2009/11/images-15.jpg)

The sapling is in a Dickensian slum, though;
Wah! Is pellucid and perky.
With so healthy broad leaves,
Roots so widely ranged,
Has unseen fruits in box up
Well be concerned in meristem.

In a jiffy, it will be in its youth
And, can bring and block storm,
Purify the defiled environment
Check the flood and landslide.
In due course the wish of the dwellers
Shall bloom and part fragrance;
Make all healthy, brave and wise.

Sure photosynthesis shall begin
To nourish the stoic en masse
Soon, make the slum dwellers nip and tuck.
Their long awaited dream-
The hellish life be over
Renewed wish shine at backwoods,
The life in dungeon enlightened.
For this to prevail;
One shouldn’t wait till doomsday
May all then, stick to true dos and don’ts.
