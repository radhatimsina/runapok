When down-pour
Wetted my face,
From the leak-hole
Of the plastic roof
And disturbed my mid-night sleep,
I recalled my dearly lost past
Recalled my hamlets duplex
Recalled the gush of tears
Of that years old bitter day.
I tried and ignored the cause
Sleep shrouded me.
Alas!
The down-pour continued
Even added to my tears
Through decade old blanket.
I found my infant wet,
Though entangled in mums bosom,
And the mum sobbing.
I twitched with ignited spirit,
And made up my mind
To find a being… to be blamed
For all the sufferings
Of the mid-night.

My mind spotted many
For the current cause
To bring all about,
Who made me hapless,
Made me homeless,
Brought me under leakage
On a deserted bank.
Thus,
I found myself a made cactus,
My infant a made waif
The mum a made stray.

The thunder outside roaring on
The down-pour continued.
With the flash of lightening
I saw my infant beside me
With half-opened mouth,
Raising cute hands
Perhaps, with questions to ask;

‘Who is enjoying my past?
Who took away my age-old pelf?
Who is responsible for unsolved ado?
How long the down-pour,
Wets us on bed?
Isnt till decay?’
