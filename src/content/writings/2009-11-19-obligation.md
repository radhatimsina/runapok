He stands still and looks towards extreme east
Looks insatiate and climbs to the roof
On raise soles spreads eyes and clutches fist
Murmurs inaudibly and whispers oof!

Jumps with caution and enters his room
Picks an old chit and reads Nineteen fifty-eight
And utters, F-1, on any norm or any form.
Looks at the photo on the wall and states Pa, you’re great.

He flexes his muscles and says, I aint a beast,
Coward, suit his name and no term can define.
He comes out, looks again towards extreme east
Points finger and speaks, Yes, there at back woods lies mine.

Coward tastes death twice, and yet, I am not,
The misery I am with must inspire me on
Let me see me be red, that’s what I ought
I’m indebted; I must pay and then own.

He reads his mothers wrinkled face…
Speaks to himself She brought me with wish,
Lost her beloved for no fault at his place,
Cared in sufferance and through me be in nice.

She lost her daughter too. She is no more
Lascivious cadets ruined her puberty
Poor ma; widowed and ousted while I was four
Im a right peg in the right hole; must end disparity.

He stares at the firmament and whispers am I the lonely sufferer?
And he roves his eyes around and says, No , they too,
Truth always prevails, we’ve got to conquer
Ma is too old, to please her and the like, I’m obliged to do.

I must bring maladies for the oppressors
To oint the oppressed’s wounds, wounds of genuine cause.
Heat shall be with the red-man and befriend the sufferers,
All chill and odds shall give the way at any cost.

Be it hell or high-water, I will strive
For victory is assured, albeit not in repose
If triumphant, we shall dance with all pride….
If ended let there be no tears in spate and deluge.

He stands still and looks towards extreme east,
Bows to Ma’s feet and narrates his obligation
Salutes Pas photo on the wall and clutches the fist
And let free his legs for an unknown destination.
