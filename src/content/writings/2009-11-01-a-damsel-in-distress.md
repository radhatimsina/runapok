The very damsel
Seen by many afore
At different venues,
Is seen again at a mart.
She is seen with stretched palms,
And she is heard by all
In same mood and tone.

She just does;
Sing and ask for alms.
‘They are there…
They are dying for you
For your comfort
For your victory
For your liberty
And its ease for all.
Please drop…
Drop, just what you can.
Dinesh and Deki breathed their last
Albeit;
With guns on arms,
For the dearth of meal.
Navin , Pema and the like marching on.
Please drop…
Drop, just what you have.
The sun shining
The following day
Shall, smile for your greatness,
And, the breeze that muffles
The hellish hell-hole
Shall breed hopes in you.
Hai my countrymen…!
I should but couldn’t
Substitute Dinesh, Deki and like
For I’m visually impaired.’
