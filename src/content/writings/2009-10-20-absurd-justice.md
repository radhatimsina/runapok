The moment the truth is
Unanimously proven fraudulent
Truthfulness fades, fails and wanes.
The possessor is tagged miscreant
Acquitted to dungeons,
Pro tem, knavish brains win bliss.

Puny, becomes the pious bearer
Face reflects his genuine dream
He smiles, with wish, while truth fades,
Keeps alive the wish at brim
Prefers witnessing own effigy on blaze,
Though alone, betwixt devilish lawyer.

Holding a morsel of soil of his whence, he whispers;
“When native land and the whence is sordidly forgotten
Self-entity ruins and young ones’ life derails,
All evil deeds nourished and well adorned,
A state of noxious gala prevails.
Evasive ploy of the tyrant be pardoned
Finally be the prey of conspiracy, astutely woven.

From under the distant graves
In skeleton, the mighty martyrs wail
From behind the iron bar
The half-dead patriots narrate True tale.
The latter breathe hope, and look far
Smell truth by degrees within and feel brave.
