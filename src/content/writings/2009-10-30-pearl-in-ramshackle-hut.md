“Since centuries they were there
From decade plus-they are here
Everyday hero dies and hero gets birth
All struggling to have and see common earth”.

I heard this from outside a ramshackle hut
As if, one was narrating a tale amidst young lot.
Leaning on the porous- stick made wall held my ear sharp
In the entire scene, single soul could I mark.

Fell into my eye a man with pen and pad
Everyone could easily read his mien, he looked sad.
I then entered to the setting with inquisitive mind
In his approach were lying volumes of vivid kind.

He ain’t at all happy with the happenings…
Narrated me of his mates and dynamic heroes’ dying
The oldies- the living account- buried in odd soil
The kind on sufferance rewarded for forefathers’ toil.

He was to write a history as of now to cure all ills
As token- For they were there as old as the hills.
I found him in lime light with apt paraphernalia
Cleared my mind, for he ain’t a man with senile de’ mentia.

Curiosity laden within me, then unmasked
Setting spoke, “He is fully compos mentis upon the task”
My mind then spoke, “Maker kudos for best made”!
For in the vicinity a misplaced leaflet read…

“There isn’t a hamlet in my country
Where, not a puny peasant is blessed martyrdom,
There isn’t a word inscribed in the history
Where, their pious ceding is mentioned”.
