Secrets and variables allow you to manage reusable configuration data. Secrets are **encrypted** and are used for sensitive data. [Learn more about encrypted secrets](https://docs.github.com/actions/automating-your-workflow-with-github-actions/creating-and-using-encrypted-secrets). Variables are shown as plain text and are used for **non-sensitive** data. [Learn more about variables](https://docs.github.com/actions/learn-github-actions/variables).
